HOLOGRAM FORGE

Type your hologram with colour codes, see it like it will look in-game, then copy the commands. Works on Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge too) with no mod or plugin.

Colours & style

Click into a line, then click a colour. &4 dark red, &6 gold, &l bold, &#ff8800 any colour. A new colour turns bold/italic off, so put &l after the colour.

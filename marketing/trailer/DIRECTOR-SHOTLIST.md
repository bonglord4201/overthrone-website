# OVERTHRONE SMP – cinematic trailer shot list

Built shot by shot from the 1-minute reference trailer you sent (19 shots, about 55 seconds), with each shot swapped for an OVERTHRONE location.
You film the shots in game, send them to me, and I edit them to match the reference: timing, colour, depth of field, music, logo and end card.

## Tools (all NeoForge 1.21.1, client-side only, nothing goes on the server)

| Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **CMDCam** (CurseForge) | Smooth camera paths. Fly to a spot, `/cam add`. Add 2–4 points, then `/cam start 8s`. The camera glides between them. This is how the reference gets its smooth moves. |
| **Iris + a shader** (Complementary Reimagined or BSL) | The warm golden look. In the shader settings turn on **Depth of Field**, and **Motion Blur** low. |
| **OBS** | Record at **1920×1080, 60 fps**. |
| **F1** | Hides the HUD, hotbar, chat and name tags. Always press it before recording. |
| **Easy NPC** (already on the server) | Background "actors": shopkeepers, guards, people standing in the market. Give them skins and poses. |
| **Friends** | Real players for the people who walk, fight and stand in the group shot. Give them nice armour or rank kits. |

Before each shot type: `/time set 23500` (sunrise) or `/time set 12500` (sunset), `/weather clear`, `/gamerule doDaylightCycle false`.

## What I do when you send the clips

- Cut them to the reference's exact rhythm, with fades and match cuts in the same places.
- Colour grade them warm and golden like the reference, add soft depth of field, slow motion where it looks good, and film grain.
- Music: an original orchestral track that builds the same way (calm → rising → big hit on the logo), so TikTok can't mute it.
- Logo reveal over shot 18, then the JOIN NOW end card.
- Two versions: **16:9** like the reference, and **9:16 vertical** for TikTok.

## Recording tips

- Keep the camera **slow**. If a move feels too slow while recording, it's about right.
- Hold still for 2 seconds at the start and end of every take.
- Film each shot 2–3 times. I'll use the best one.
- Name the files by shot number (`shot01.mp4`, `shot02.mp4` …) so I know where each goes.

# The Hunter's Ledger: 1,012 coin quests

Rewards are Lightman's Currency coins (10 Copper = 1 Iron, 10 Iron = 1 Gold, 10 Gold = 1 Emerald, 10 Emerald = 1 Diamond, 10 Diamond = 1 Netherite).
Doing every quest pays about **1,500 Netherite** in total. Every 10th entry and each finale also gives enchanted gear. Each Ledger unlocks after entry 50 of the one before it.

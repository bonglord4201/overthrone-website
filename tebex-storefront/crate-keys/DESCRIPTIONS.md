# Package descriptions (paste one into each Tebex package)

## 1x Armor Crate Key

```
1x Armor Crate Key

You get 1 Armor Key.

Gear up for the throne. Armor Crate keys let you choose the exact armor piece you want: no luck needed.

What you can redeem at the Armor Crate:
• Throne Guard Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings & Boots: Protection IX, Blast Protection IX, Fire Protection IX, Projectile Protection IX, Unbreaking VIII, Mending (3 keys each)
• Monarch's Wings (Elytra): Unbreaking VIII, Mending (2 keys)
• Bastion Shield: Unbreaking VIII, Mending (2 keys)
• Abyssal Helm: Respiration VIII, Aqua Affinity, Thorns III, Unbreaking VIII, Mending (4 keys)
• Shadowstep Leggings: Swift Sneak III, Thorns III, Unbreaking VIII, Mending (4 keys)
• Frostbound Boots: Frost Walker X, Feather Falling IX, Thorns III, Unbreaking VIII, Mending (4 keys)

How it works:
1. Go to the crates: /warp crates
2. Click the Armor Crate to open it.
3. Pick the reward you want and left-click it. The keys are taken and the item goes straight into your inventory.

Delivery: your keys arrive as items the next time you're online (within about a minute).
```

How do these color dice work?

Every roll is made on our server, not in your browser. When you press Roll again!, the server picks a color for each die, and each of the 6 colors has exactly the same chance: 1 in 6. All the dice can land on the same color.

The possible colors are Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Purple. You can roll 1 to 6 dice at once, switch to number dice, and pick any theme you like.

Each roll is saved with an ID and the exact time before you see it, and the site has no way to change a roll after it's made. To check a streamer's roll, press Verify History and type the ID shown under their dice. You'll see the official result, when it was rolled, and the rolls made just before and after it.

About us

ColorRoll is a free online color dice roller for color game streams, parties, board games and any other game of chance. There's nothing to sign up for or download.

We built it so viewers never have to take a streamer's word for a roll. Every result can be checked by anyone, any time.

About ColorRoll

What ColorRoll is and why we built it.

ColorRoll is a free online color dice roller. You can roll 1 to 6 dice, switch between color dice and number dice, pick from a range of themes, and keep a running count — with nothing to sign up for and nothing to download.

Rolls anyone can verify

Every roll is made on our server, not in your browser, and saved with an ID and the exact time before you ever see it. We have no way to change a roll after it is made. Anyone can press Verify, type the ID shown under a roller's dice, and see the official result and the rolls made just before and after it. We built ColorRoll so viewers never have to simply take someone's word for a roll.

Fair by design

Each die is fair: every color has the same 1-in-6 chance, every roll is independent of the last, and all the dice can land on the same color. We do not bias, filter, or edit results.

Who we are

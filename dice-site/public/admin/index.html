Catch cheats

Look up a roller by their ID or a roll ID, see their re-rolls, then flag or ban them. The dice are never touched — a flag just warns players, and a ban stops that roller from rolling here.

A roller ID is 12 characters (a–f and numbers). A roll ID is 10 letters and numbers.
